24-hour solar ring

Dashes, dots and segments align to clock-time divisions. Solid is continuous. Golden-hour overlays keep their separate colour.

Editing a solar time enables manual solar times. Turn it off to restore calculated values. Moon calculations remain independent.

Solar times follow the selected date and location when calculations are enabled. The 24-hour scale replaces compass degrees to avoid overlapping labels. Golden arcs overlay the twilight/day arcs around sunrise and sunset.

Sun & moon

Diameter changes the rise/set arc and its endpoint markers together, up to the 454 px screen diameter. At the edge, strokes may be clipped by the circular screen.

The inner arc shows when the moon is above the horizon on the selected local day. Both ticks point inward. Filled endpoint = rise; hollow endpoint = set. Uses the same midnight/noon orientation as the outer scale.

Azimuth markers compensate for watch heading. A hollow moon is below the horizon; calculated positions follow your location and preview time.

North compass

0° = facing north, 90° = east. The N needle turns opposite your heading to point north. Simulated watch orientation; continuous live tracking requires Garmin API validation.

Layers

Expanded design catalogue. Sensor availability depends on Garmin’s watch-face APIs; “verify API” entries are requested concepts, not promised live fields. Sample values unless calculated below.

Y-axis mode links the left and right bubbles. Upper and lower pairs remain independent. Drag either group or use these controls.

Coordinate systems · how placement works

Screen: top-left is (0, 0); centre is (227, 227). X increases right; Y increases down. The vertical Y-axis runs through the centre.

Layout angles: 0° right, 90° down, 180° left, 270° / −90° up. Radius is distance from the centre. Upper-pair angle is measured upward from horizontal; lower-pair angle downward. Y-axis mirror: X′ = 454 − X, Y′ = Y.

Compass / clock angles: 0° at the top and increase clockwise. These are separate from layout angles. Axes rotate with the watch preview. The 454-unit canvas spans the 38 mm screen.

Click to edit. Ctrl/⌘-click selects multiple; Alt-click selects underneath overlaps.
